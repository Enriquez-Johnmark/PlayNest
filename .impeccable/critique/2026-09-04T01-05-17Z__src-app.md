## Design Specificity Verdict

The warm visual language is coherent, but the behavior acts like a lifestyle/membership platform rather than a calm parent booking app. The deterministic detector found no code-pattern violations, but it cannot measure information density, false affordances, or navigation depth.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Pretend commercial checkout

The review screen shows payment, vouchers, credits, taxes, and membership benefits although this local demo neither collects payment nor manages those services. Replace it with a truthful booking review and one `Confirm booking` action.

### [P0] Success does not deliberately land in My Bookings

The parent should finish at the saved booking. Make `View booking` the confirmation primary action and `Back to home` secondary; remove stack-back framing from confirmation.

### [P1] Activities hide the primary interaction and use inconsistent CTA copy

The visible feed card is not tappable; a separate info icon opens details while CTAs vary between booking, selecting a time, claiming a place, and joining a waitlist. Make the entire card open details and use one factual CTA path.

### [P1] Home is a content hub, not a clear start state

Upcoming check-in, search, filters, a spotlight, quick-book cards, editorial content, and a quote compete above the fold. Keep greeting, conditional next booking, and at most two discovery modules. Put search and filters in Activities.

### [P2] Branding and Profile scope drift

Bookings uses a house glyph instead of the shared PlayNest mark, while Profile promises memberships, payment, health/care management, messaging, and notifications that are mostly previews. Use one header mark and shrink Profile to demo-family facts and booking-relevant settings.

## Positive Findings

The app has a polished warm palette and readable major headings. Its actual booking engine correctly persists a local booking, and validates child eligibility, capacity, session time, and duplicate booking prevention.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should Home prioritize the next booking when one exists, and discovery only when none exists?
2. Should the milestone remain an explicit no-payment reservation demo?
3. Should every path use activity card, activity detail, session, child, review, confirmation, then My Bookings?
4. Should Profile retain only parent/child details and one booking-relevant care note?
